# Compliance Checklist

Use this reference when the user asks about booking overseas cloud or SaaS costs in a mainland China company, likely tax or bank review points, or what materials should be kept on file.

## Mainland company booking overseas cloud and SaaS costs

When the user asks whether a Shanghai or mainland company can book Tencent Cloud International, Cloudflare, or similar overseas service fees:

- Say that such costs can usually be booked if they are genuine business expenses tied to company operations.
- Explain that being bookable does not automatically mean input VAT can be credited.
- Explain that overseas suppliers often provide invoice, bill, or statement records instead of PRC VAT invoices.
- Keep the distinction clear:
  - accounting entry may be possible
  - VAT credit is usually limited
  - enterprise income tax deductibility depends on the support files and facts

## Typical review points

When the user asks whether authorities may review the details:

- Explain that review can come from:
  - tax authorities
  - banks processing cross-border payments
  - FX authenticity review
  - annual audit
  - financing or transaction due diligence
- Explain that the usual focus is:
  - whether the transaction is real
  - what service was actually purchased
  - whether the payer, user, and contract party line up
  - whether withholding or other tax treatment was handled correctly

Avoid describing this as routine deep technical line-by-line inspection unless there is a large amount, inconsistent paperwork, or another abnormal signal.

## Response for evasion requests

If the user asks how to avoid supervision or checks:

- Refuse to provide evasion methods.
- Redirect to lawful ways to reduce friction:
  - choose the right contracting entity up front
  - keep support files complete
  - align the billing entity with the actual service user
  - review tax handling before payment or filing

## Tencent Cloud International invoicing

When the user asks whether Tencent Cloud International can issue a mainland invoice:

- Explain that Tencent Cloud International usually follows an invoice route rather than mainland PRC VAT invoicing.
- Explain that Tencent Cloud mainland site supports mainland VAT invoicing, including digital invoices.
- Suggest checking:
  - which site the order was placed on
  - which entity is named in the contract or console
  - whether the billing center says invoice or VAT invoice
  - what currency and format the invoice record shows

## Support file checklist

Offer this list when the user wants a practical checklist:

- contract, service terms, or order record
- invoice, bill, or monthly statement
- payment proof
- supplier entity information
- backend usage screenshots or console exports
- memo describing business purpose
- Chinese translation for key foreign-language documents when needed
- internal approval or procurement record when the company uses one

## Practical caution

Do not give categorical legal conclusions. Use wording such as:

- `通常可以入账`
- `能入账不等于能抵扣进项税`
- `是否可税前扣除、是否涉及代扣代缴，要结合合同主体和服务内容判断`
