# Diligence, Banking, and Accounting Checklist

Use this reference when the user asks what to prepare for bank onboarding, advisor review, bookkeeping setup, audit support, or general diligence for a Shanghai parent and Hong Kong subsidiary structure.

## What this checklist is for

This checklist helps the user prepare a coherent file set for:

- Hong Kong company secretary or incorporation service providers
- Hong Kong bank account opening or account review
- Stripe or other payment processor onboarding
- mainland and Hong Kong accountants
- tax advisors
- annual audit
- investor or transaction due diligence

## 1. Corporate structure files

Ask the user to prepare:

- Shanghai parent business license and basic company information
- shareholder structure chart
- Hong Kong subsidiary incorporation documents
- director and shareholder registers
- UBO information
- board resolutions or shareholder resolutions for key setup steps

Useful wording:

`先把股权结构、董事信息和最终受益人信息整理清楚，后面的银行、会计、支付机构基本都会反复问这套。`

## 2. Business narrative files

Ask the user to prepare:

- one-page business description
- product or service summary
- website links and screenshots
- target market and customer type summary
- explanation of why a Hong Kong entity is commercially needed

The core goal is to make the story consistent across banks, Stripe, accountants, and tax advisors.

Useful wording:

`很多卡点不是因为结构本身不行，而是每个材料里对“谁卖给谁、谁收款、谁提供服务”的说法不一致。`

## 3. Revenue flow files

If the Hong Kong company will collect overseas income, ask for:

- customer terms or sample contracts
- website terms of service and privacy policy
- Stripe account information
- settlement bank account details
- refund policy
- sample invoices or billing pages

Check that the contract party, merchant entity, and bank account point to the same entity.

## 4. Cost and supplier files

If the Hong Kong company will pay Tencent Cloud Hong Kong, Cloudflare, or other overseas SaaS vendors, ask for:

- vendor account screenshots
- billing entity details
- invoices, bills, or statements
- payment method details
- short memo explaining business use

Useful wording:

`费用谁承担、服务谁在用、账单抬头是谁，最好提前统一，不然后面会计和银行都会追着补解释。`

## 5. Intercompany files

If Shanghai supports the Hong Kong entity, ask for:

- intercompany service agreement
- service scope summary
- pricing method note
- cost allocation support
- invoice or settlement process design

Always explain that this is the area where transfer pricing questions often arise.

## 6. Accounting and tax files

Ask the user to prepare or confirm:

- chart of accounts approach
- monthly bookkeeping process
- invoice and support document archive rules
- Hong Kong audit and tax filing calendar
- mainland tax handling for related-party flows
- dividend or remittance path assumptions

Useful wording:

`更稳的做法是先把月度记账和年度审计路径设计好，再去开账号和走收付款。`

## 7. Bank and payment processor questions to anticipate

Tell the user to expect questions like:

- your business model
- your target customers and countries
- expected monthly turnover
- source of funds
- reason for the Hong Kong entity
- relationship between the Shanghai and Hong Kong entities
- whether the website and contracts match the merchant entity

## 8. Practical delivery format

If the user wants a very actionable suggestion, recommend packaging materials into:

- one structure chart
- one business narrative memo
- one revenue and cost flow memo
- one intercompany memo
- one folder of corporate and billing evidence

## Tone and caution

- Be practical and checklist-oriented.
- Emphasize consistency and readiness.
- Do not promise that having the checklist guarantees account opening, tax acceptance, or audit outcomes.
